## Purpose
The full-screen reading flow approved in S-2: one level at a time, each prerequisite behind a centred question, the main concept last.

## Requirements
1. Opens full screen with no app name, toolbar or list of levels. Only the main concept indicator (bottom left) is always shown.
2. Each prerequisite starts on its cover: the question alone, centred, with Yes and No under it.
3. Yes slides the next page in from the right (the next cover, or the main concept).
4. No reveals the level: title, paragraph, figure, and a Next button at the bottom right. The page asks no further question.
5. Next moves to the next cover or to the main concept. The main concept page has no Next.
6. A document with no prerequisites opens straight on the main concept.
7. Keyboard: Y and N answer a cover, Right arrow or Enter is Next, Esc closes any open popup.
8. Fits any window from 1024 x 640 up: the figure scales to the width between the page margins without cutting off any part.

## Out of scope
- Paragraph terms and references; figure parts; motion; themes (each its own spec).

## Acceptance checks
- Walking the example: answering Yes on L1 shows L2's cover; No on L2 shows L2 revealed; Next shows the main concept with no Next button.
- The same flow works by keyboard only.
- At 1024 x 640 and 1920 x 1080 no figure part is clipped.
