## Purpose
Turning a hard concept into an ADE document is the real work; this makes it repeatable.

## Requirements
1. Given a concept, proposes the prerequisite levels (0 to 2) and their cover questions, for the user to accept or edit.
2. Writes each level's paragraph (about 5 lines), marks every term a newcomer may not know, and writes each term's definition.
3. Designs each figure from the kit's vocabulary, with tags, 5 to 8 word captions, connection notes and at most 3 moving parts.
4. Attaches real sources to every term, part explainer and reference, and flags any claim it could not source.
5. Runs the validator and fixes every failure before handing the document over.

## Out of scope
- A visual editor.
