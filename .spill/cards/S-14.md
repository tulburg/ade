## Purpose
A reader who answers No on a level does not understand its basics, so they cannot understand the levels built on them either. Asking again on the next cover is a wasted question.

## Requirements
1. Once the reader answers No on any cover, every later cover is skipped: Next goes straight to the next level's revealed page (paragraph and figure), then to the main concept.
2. A Yes keeps today's flow: the next level's cover is still asked.
3. Back from a page reached this way goes to the previous page, never to the skipped cover.
4. The indicator and keyboard (Y, N, arrows, Enter) keep working; Y and N do nothing on a page with no cover.

## Out of scope
- Changing the cover questions or the number of levels.

## Acceptance checks
- In dist/gps.html: No on L1, then Next, shows the L2 page with no L2 cover; Next again shows the main concept.
- Yes on L1 still shows the L2 cover.
- Back from the L2 page returns to the L1 page.
- A reader test covers both paths.
