## Purpose
Every part that needs explaining can be opened in place, so the figure and its explanation are read together.

## Requirements
1. Clickable parts show their blue tag in the top-left or top-right corner, and their caption next to it; the blue outline appears only on hover, focus, or while open.
2. Clicking a part opens its explainer as a popup on the part with an arrow pointing at it: tag, name, what it is, how it connects (tag chips), and sources.
3. A tag chip for a part on the same level highlights and opens that part. A chip for another level (`L1 A2`) opens that level's figure on that part, and Back returns to where the reader was.
4. Tab moves through the parts in tag order; Enter opens, Esc closes.
5. Each part has an accessible name made of its tag and caption.

## Out of scope
- Drawing the parts (figure kit).

## Acceptance checks
- In the example main concept, C2 opens the Context window explainer pointing at C2; its L2 B1 chip opens L2 at B1 and Back returns to C2.
- All parts are reachable and openable with the keyboard.
