## Purpose
Motion shows how the concept works over time (a token being picked and appended, a tool call travelling), limited to 3 parts so the eye can follow.

## Requirements
1. A figure plays its moving parts (at most 3) in a loop once the figure is on screen; they are the only red parts and carry a numbered marker in play order.
2. The motion of each part is the one described in the document (for example: a token travels from the odds to the end of the tape).
3. A pause / play control sits with the figure; motion pauses while any popup is open.
4. With the system 'reduce motion' setting on, figures start paused and show the final frame.

## Out of scope
- Motion inside popups or the paragraph.

## Acceptance checks
- Both example figures loop their 3 moving parts in marker order.
- With reduce motion on, nothing moves until play is pressed.
