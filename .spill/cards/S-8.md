## Purpose
Readers open documents at any hour; both themes from S-2 are supported.

## Requirements
1. Light and dark themes use the S-2 palettes: monochrome inks, red for moving parts only, blue for clickable and links only.
2. The reader follows the system setting by default and offers a toggle.
3. Every element (paragraph, terms, popups, figure, buttons) has both themes; text meets WCAG AA contrast.

## Out of scope
- Custom or per-document themes.

## Acceptance checks
- Every page of the example looks right in both themes, checked by screenshot.
- A contrast check passes for body text, captions, notes and tags in both themes.
