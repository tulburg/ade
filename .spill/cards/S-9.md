## Purpose
Draws a document's figures in the approved S-2 look, so every ADE document looks like one family without hand-drawing each figure.

## Requirements
1. Each part in the document has a kind from the kit's vocabulary and an explicit position and size on the figure's grid (D-5: 1000 units wide, as in S-2); the kit draws it there and never moves it. Renders the figure as SVG, flat 2D front view, in the S-2 style: thin monochrome inks, engraved name plates, small precise parts.
2. Provides the part vocabulary used in S-2: panels, token chips, layer stacks, bars, dials, lists, file icons, terminal, pipes with flow, and notes on connections.
3. Places each clickable part's tag in its top-left or top-right corner (outside only when the part is under 60 x 40), and its caption inside next to the tag in the soft text colour.
4. Exposes each part's bounds so the reader can draw hover outlines and point popups at it.
5. Draws the moving parts in red with their numbered markers, and gives the reader what it needs to animate them.
6. Renders both themes from the same data.

## Out of scope
- Interaction and animation playback (reader specs).

## Acceptance checks
- The two S-2 figures, rebuilt from document data with the kit, match the approved artboards side by side.
- A part smaller than 60 x 40 gets its tag outside its corner.
