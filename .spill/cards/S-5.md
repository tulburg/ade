## Purpose
ADE reads like a dictionary: a reader at any level must understand every word on the page. The paragraph explains the level in about 5 lines and opens a full definition for every hard word.

## Requirements
1. The paragraph is justified and spans the page between the margins, about 5 lines at the default size.
2. Terms are underlined in the link blue. Clicking or tapping one opens its definition under the word, with an arrow pointing at it: the term, its explanation, the figure part it maps to (as a tag chip), and its sources.
3. Clicking a part chip in a definition highlights that part in the figure (blue outline) and opens its explainer.
4. References show as small numbers ([1]). Clicking one opens a popup with that source: title, kind and link. A reference never wraps onto a line apart from the word before it.
5. One popup is open at a time; clicking outside or Esc closes it.
6. Popups stay inside the window: they flip above the word or shift sideways near an edge.

## Out of scope
- How definitions are written (authoring).

## Acceptance checks
- In the example, every term in the L1 paragraph opens its definition, and the A3 chip in 'attention' highlights A3.
- A term on the last line and one at the right edge open popups that are fully visible.
