## Purpose
The first real ADE document and the one to share: a general topic most people use every day but cannot explain, using all 3 levels. It is also the fixture the reader, kit and export are checked against.

Topic: **How your phone knows where you are (GPS)**. Everyone uses it; almost nobody knows it works by timing radio signals with atomic clocks, and that it would drift by kilometres a day without Einstein's corrections.

## Requirements
1. Three levels, the maximum: L1 and L2 as prerequisites, the topic as the main concept.
   - L1 **Distance from travel time**: a signal moving at a known speed tells you how far away its sender is from how long it took (radio moves at light speed, so a millionth of a second is 300 m).
   - L2 **Finding a point from distances (trilateration)**: one distance puts you on a circle (a sphere in 3D), two narrow it to two points, three to one.
   - Main **How GPS finds where you are**: satellites broadcast their position and the exact time from atomic clocks; the phone times four of them (the fourth fixes its own cheap clock), solves for its position, and relativity corrections keep it from drifting about 10 km a day.
2. Each level has its cover question, a ~5 line paragraph with every hard word as a defined term, numbered references, and a figure in the approved S-2 style with tagged parts, 5 to 8 word captions, connection notes and at most 3 moving parts.
3. Every figure is drawn and approved in Spill on this spec before it is written into the document.
4. Every term, part explainer and reference has a real, reputable source (for example gps.gov, NASA, physics textbooks or university pages).
