## Purpose
User feedback on the GPS document: moving parts go by too fast to read (C3 in the GPS main figure), and the figures are cramped: text runs into lines, tags sit against part edges, and a list slot spills out of its box. Clean both up in the spirit of Emil Kowalski's craft: unhurried motion with soft easing, and small, consistent spacing so every label has room.

## Requirements
1. **Slower motion.** Each moving part's movement takes about 2.5 s (now 1.1 s), with an ease-in-out curve, and holds for about 1.5 s before the next one starts; the whole loop rests about 2 s before repeating. A traveling chip moves at a steady, readable speed (no faster than about 250 grid units a second), so a long path takes longer than a short one.
2. **Tag inset.** Tags sit at least 12 units in from the part's edges (now 7 to 8), never touching a border.
3. **Reserved caption space.** A caption never overlaps its part's content or a title bar: it sits on its own line just above the part (or below with captionBelow), 8 units clear of the border, and the kit reserves that space when placing it.
4. **Notes clear of lines.** Connection notes sit at least 6 units from any line, so no text crosses a path.
5. **Content fits its part.** The kit sizes list slots, terminal lines and similar content to fit inside the part with at least 12 units of padding; the validator fails a part whose content would overflow (for example 4 list items in a box only tall enough for 3).
6. **Redraw.** The GPS document and the AI agent fixture are re-laid out under these rules and their figures are drawn on this spec for approval before closing.

## Out of scope
- New part kinds or changes to the page layout outside the figures.

## Acceptance checks
- In dist/gps.html, C3's signal visibly takes about 2.5 s or more to travel to the phone and can be read on the way.
- No tag, caption or note in either document touches a border or crosses a line (checked on the redrawn figures).
- The validator flags a list with more items than its part can hold; a test covers it.
- The redrawn figures are approved on the board.
