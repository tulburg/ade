Pin 3 removed Prev/Next ('it's a reader'), D-2 then added Next after the reveal. Going back is not decided, and S-6's cross-level chips need some way to return.

- A. No back; only a cross-level jump offers a one-time Back to where you were (recommended)
- B. Left arrow or swipe goes back, no visible button
- C. A small Back button next to Next
