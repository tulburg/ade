This decides how much layout work the author does and how much the kit does. The S-2 figures were placed by hand on a 1000-unit grid.

- A. Parts with explicit positions on a grid; the kit draws them (recommended: matches S-2, predictable)
- B. Parts and connections only; the kit lays them out automatically
- C. Raw SVG per figure, with tags marked on its elements
