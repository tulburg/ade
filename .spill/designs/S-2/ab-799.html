ADE illustration language
Marks, motion and rules
Marks
Simple part
Needs no explaining. Thin mid-grey outline, no ring, no tag.
Clickable part
A blue reference tag inside its top-left or top-right corner (outside only when the part is too small); a blue outline appears on hover. The tag is its address: other explainers cite it as B2 or, across levels, L2 B2.
Open part
The part whose explainer is open: solid blue tag, heavier outline. Its explainer pops up on the part, with an arrow.
Moving part
The only colour in the figure. Numbered 1 to 3; never more than 3 per figure.
Motion in Fig 1 (L1)
1 · Tape advances (A1)
Each step the tape shifts left one cell and the new token lands in the empty slot. 600 ms, eased.
2 · Odds rise (A4)
Bars grow from zero to their probability after the model runs. The winning bar is the accent.
3 · Token returns (pipe)
The picked token rides the pipe back to the tape, closing the loop. Then step 1 repeats.
Rules
01At most 3 levels: up to 2 prerequisites, then the main concept, which is the document title. Only the current level is shown; the main concept is always named.
02Each level opens with one question. Yes moves on; No shows its illustration.
03At most 3 moving parts per figure, and they are the only colour.
04Every part that needs explaining is clickable and tagged; simple parts are not.
05Names live on engraved plates on the part, never as floating labels or leader lines.
06Every explainer lists its sources: links, PDFs, docs.
07Motion is eased and loops slowly when idle; it stops on hover and with reduced motion.
